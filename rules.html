🏆 How It Works

Each player picks teams each round and earns points based on their results. The player with the most points at the end of the tournament wins. Pick wisely — each team can only be used once across the entire competition.

You have a unique personal link to access your dashboard, submit picks, and track your score. The live leaderboard and match centre are available to everyone — no login needed.

🔵 Group Stages

There are three group phases across 11–27 June, covering all 72 matches of the group stage. Each phase you pick two teams: one as your 3-point pick and one as your 2-point pick. A win earns those points, a draw earns 1pt regardless of pick type, a loss earns 0. The 3-point pick is only available in group stages.

🔴 Knockout Stages

From the Round of 32 through to the Final, you pick one team per round. All knockout picks are worth 2 points for a win. Scoring for each outcome:

  • Your team wins — 2pts (+ longshot bonus if applicable)
  • Your team wins in Extra Time or on Penalties — 1pt (+ longshot bonus if applicable) — you stay in
  • Your team loses in Extra Time or on Penalties — 1pt — but you're eliminated
  • Your team loses — 0pts — and you're eliminated

⭐ Longshot Bonus

Typically around 20 teams, determined by tournament odds, are designated as longshots, marked with ⭐ in the pick form. If a longshot team wins — including in Extra Time or on Penalties in knockout rounds — the player who picked them earns an extra +1 point on top. The bonus applies in both group and knockout stages.

💀 Elimination

You are eliminated in any of the following situations:

  • Your picked team loses in the knockout phase (including in extra time or on penalties)
  • You have used all remaining teams and cannot make any more picks
  • You miss a knockout deadline and there are no default pool teams left that are eligible for you

In all cases, your score is frozen at the point where your final pick was used.

Example: In Euro 24, Lee won his semi-final pick, but had no eligible teams left for the final — he was eliminated after the semi-final and his score was frozen there.

🎯 Goals Prediction

At the start of the competition, each player submits a prediction for the total number of goals scored across all 104 matches, including goals in extra time. This is used as a tiebreaker — the player closest to the actual total wins the tiebreak. You can set or update your prediction from your personal dashboard before the Group Phase 1 deadline.

🔔 Deadline Reminders

Want a nudge 1 hour before the deadline if you haven't picked yet? Enable notifications from your personal dashboard — there's a prompt below your stats.

iPhone users: notifications only work when the app is added to your home screen. In Safari, tap the Share button → Add to Home Screen, then open it from there and enable notifications. One-time setup.

Android users: just tap Enable notifications in Chrome — no home screen step needed.

🔮 The Oracle

Not sure who to pick? The Oracle is a built-in advisor on your personal dashboard. It assesses over a quadrillion possible pick combinations and uses an algorithm — with a random element — to suggest the most suitable team for your situation, rated ⭐ to ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐.

Picks made via the Oracle are marked with 🔮. For entertainment only — the Oracle accepts no responsibility for your leaderboard position!

🔢 Tiebreakers

If players are level on points at any stage, the following tiebreakers apply in order:

  • LMS — furthest round reached
    The player who has survived to the furthest knockout round ranks higher.
  • Goal difference from picked games
    Cumulative goal difference from all matches involving your picked teams. Higher is better.
  • Total goals prediction
    Closest to the actual total goals scored in the tournament (including extra time). Submitted at the start of the competition. Lower difference wins.

💰 Entry & Prizes

It costs £10 to enter. Hand it over however you like — cash, bank transfer, carrier pigeon. Just make sure it arrives.

The pot is every entry fee combined. Prizes scale with the number of players.

The worst performer in the Round of 32 gets their £10 entry fee back. Ranked by points, then goal difference, then goals prediction proximity. If it's still a tie — they split it. Tracked live on the Spoon tab on the leaderboard during the Round of 32.

🏆 Last Man Standing

Whoever gets eliminated last — i.e. survives the longest — wins £10. If two or more of you are eliminated at exactly the same time, you split it evenly. This one's for anyone mid-table: you don't need a leaderboard run to win something here, just outlast everyone else. Tracked live on the Prizes tab on the leaderboard.

A fun stat — no prize attached. The 🪦 pill marks whoever was first eliminated in the knockout phase.

Teams & Banding

How longshot teams were designated

A team earns ⭐ longshot status only if they met both criteria before the tournament started: long enough outright winner odds (Paddy Power UK), and a minimum odds threshold from their group stage match odds. Both conditions had to be satisfied. The list was fixed at tournament start and never updated — a team stays a ⭐ longshot even if they go on a run. Most ⭐ teams sit in the 🔴 No Hoper band. Don't confuse the two — "Long Shot" is a band label, ⭐ is the bonus flag. Related, but stored independently, and they can differ.

What the banding system is

Every team is assigned one of four bands: 🔵 Favourite · 🟡 Dark Horse · 🟠 Long Shot · 🔴 No Hoper. Set manually before the tournament based on pre-tournament outright odds. Banding has zero direct effect on points — it's used by The Oracle and shown on your pick form so you know what you're dealing with.

How your eligible team pool works

At any point, the teams available to pick are: all 48 teams, minus any you've already used in a previous round, minus any teams eliminated from the tournament. This refreshes automatically — so as teams get knocked out, your pool shrinks. Choose carefully early on; burning a good team in the group stage means you can't use them in the knockouts.

The Leaderboard

What the coloured circles mean

Each circle in a player's form strip represents one pick. The colour is the outcome; the number inside is the points earned for that pick — including whether it was a 3-point or 2-point group pick, plus any longshot bonus.

  • 🟢 Green — a win. Includes wins in extra time or on penalties in the knockout rounds.
  • 🟡 Amber — a group stage draw.
  • 🔴 Red — a loss. Includes losing in extra time or on penalties in the knockout rounds.

So a circle showing "1" in red means a knockout pens loss — you earned the point, but you're out.

What the movement arrows mean

The ▲▼ arrows next to a player's position show movement since the end of the previous round — not since yesterday, not since this morning. They're based on leaderboard snapshots taken when the last result of each round is saved. Mid-round, they'll look static or odd — that's expected.

What the 🏆 trophy badge means

Awarded to the player with the highest points in a given round — Pick of the Round. Tiebroken by goal difference from that round's picks. If still tied, the badge is shared. Bragging rights only, no extra points.

What the flag next to a player's name means

In the group stages, flag(s) next to a player's name on the main table show which team(s) they picked this round, once the deadline has passed. A gold underline = 3-point pick; a grey underline = 2-point pick. A double underline (either colour) means that team is a longshot. Tap the flag to see the team name and result.

What ✔ and ✕ mean next to a name

In the knockout phase, ✔ means that player's pick for the current round has a confirmed result. ✕ means they've been eliminated. The ✔ clears when the next round begins.

What the small number in a Matrix cell means

Each cell in the Matrix shows a result letter (W, D, L or ?) plus a small subscript showing which round that team was used — GP for group phases, then 32, 16, QF, SF, F for knockout rounds. It's there so you can quickly see not just that a team was used, but when. Useful for understanding how far back someone burned a key pick.

What "Good Left" means in the Matrix

The Good Left column counts how many Favourites and Dark Horses you still have available — your quality picks. Total Left is all eligible teams including Long Shots and No Hopers. A player with a high Total but low Good Left is running on fumes.

The Oracle

How it actually makes decisions

The Oracle runs a decision tree against your remaining eligible teams and the current round's fixtures. It isn't random — but there is a random element within each tier if multiple teams qualify at the same priority level. Priority order:

  • 1. Longshot vs No Hoper — always the preferred pick if available
  • 2. Stronger longshot vs weaker longshot (by odds)
  • 3. Late KO (Quarter Finals onwards) — deploy a Favourite
  • 4. Group stage — Dark Horse is the sweet spot
  • 5. Any KO round — Favourite
  • 6. Nothing fits — damage limitation (best available)

The star rating (⭐–⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐) reflects how confident the Oracle is in the tier it landed on. A five-star suggestion means it found a textbook pick. One star means it's guessing.

Missed Picks & the Default Pool

How auto-assignment works

The 16 pool teams were selected as roughly the middle tier of the field by pre-tournament odds — not good enough to hand you an advantage, not bad enough to feel like a punishment. Ordered alphabetically — objective, if slightly unfortunate for Austria. At the moment of assignment, eliminated teams are skipped and teams you've already used are skipped. For group phases, the 3-point pick is assigned first, then the 2-point pick, from the next eligible team down the list. For knockout rounds, one pick is assigned; if no eligible pool team remains, you're eliminated. Auto-assigned picks are flagged on your player page so you know which ones weren't your choice.

Deadlines & the Day Window

Why the day window is noon–noon BST

The tournament is hosted in the United States, so matches kick off in the afternoon and evening American time — which broadly maps to late evening and overnight UK time. The noon–noon BST window means a "day" of picks covers all matches in a natural American match day, without awkwardly splitting across midnight. It also gives you the morning to pick after seeing overnight results.

Under the Hood

How elimination fires

Two routes: (1) your team loses a knockout match — at full time, in extra time, or on penalties; (2) you have no eligible teams left with no pending results outstanding. The eliminated flag on your player record is the sole source of truth. It's never re-derived from pick history on the fly — if it says you're in, you're in.

How goal difference is tracked

Every goal in the tournament counts toward your GD total — including extra time goals. Penalty shootout goals don't count. GD is tiebreaker #2, after furthest round reached.

What "furthest round reached" means

You only reach a round if you made a valid pick in it. It's tiebreaker #1. You can't claim credit for a round you coasted through without picking.

What the goals prediction tracks

Your prediction is for the total goals scored across all 104 matches — including extra time goals, excluding penalty shootout goals. The 3rd place match counts. Closest to the actual total wins tiebreaker #3. You can update your prediction anytime before the Group Phase 1 deadline.
